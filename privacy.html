Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Learn how AMARA Symphora handles information when you use the Bot.

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

01. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AMARA Symphora ("AMARA", "the Bot", or "the Service") may collect, use, and handle information when you interact with the Bot on Discord.

By using AMARA, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

02. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, AMARA may process the following information:

  • Discord identifiers: User IDs, server IDs, and channel IDs needed to operate Bot features.
  • Server configuration: Settings configured by authorized server administrators.
  • Command information: Commands and interactions submitted to AMARA to provide requested functionality.
  • Feature-related information: Data needed for features such as music, leveling, tickets, giveaways, and moderation.
  • Technical information: Error logs and diagnostic information used to monitor service reliability.

The information processed depends on the features enabled and used within a Discord server.

03. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate AMARA's features.
  • Maintain server configurations and settings.
  • Support music and community functionality.
  • Diagnose errors and improve service reliability.
  • Protect the Bot and its users against misuse.

04. Data Storage & Retention

Certain feature-related information may be stored using database services to support persistent functionality.

Technical logs may also be retained for operational monitoring and troubleshooting.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, feature requirements, and operational needs.

05. Data Sharing

AMARA does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers when necessary to operate the Bot, such as hosting, database, audio, and Discord services.

These providers may handle information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

06. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by AMARA against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

07. User Requests

If you have questions or requests concerning information associated with your use of AMARA, please contact the AMARA team through our official community server.

We may need relevant Discord identifiers or additional details to locate the information related to your request.

08. Children's Privacy

AMARA is a general-purpose Discord bot and is not designed to collect personal information specifically from children.

Users should follow Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable local laws when accessing Discord and its services.

09. Third-Party Services

AMARA operates through Discord and may depend on other third-party services. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and practices.

We encourage users to review the relevant third-party privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AMARA's features, data practices, or operational requirements change.

Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the AMARA team through our official community server.
